# Focus Grid - User Guide

Focus Grid is a premium productivity tool designed to help you clear your mind, prioritize your most important work, and maintain deep focus without the clutter of a traditional todo list.

## 🎯 What is it for?
Focus Grid is built on the philosophy of **"The Dump & The Focus"**. 
Most people feel overwhelmed because their minds are full of unfinished loops. Focus Grid provides a safe place to "dump" those thoughts (Venting Area) and then select exactly **one** thing to work on right now (Now View).

---

## 🚀 How to Use the System

### 1. The Mentality (The Flow)
1. **CAPTURE**: Use "The Dump" or the **Quick Capture (+)** button to get everything out of your head.
2. **PLAN**: Go to the **Planning** view to organize those thoughts into Tasks and assign them to Domains (Work, Personal, etc.).
3. **FOCUS**: Select a task as your "Now" focus. All other distractions are hidden.
4. **REVIEW**: At the end of the day, look at your stats in the **Review** tab to see your progress.

### 2. UI Controls
- **Floating (+) Button**: Use this for lightning-fast capture of a task or note from anywhere.
- **Planning Tab**: Your command center. Clean up your inbox, set priorities, and decide what is "Today's" focus.
- **Now View (Default)**: Where the work happens. Once you start a task, a timer tracks your session.
- **Review Tab**: A summary of your deep work sessions and completed goals.

## 💡 Top Scenarios

### Scenario A: The "Morning Brain Dump"
You wake up feeling anxious about 20 different things.
- **Action**: Open Focus Grid and type everything into "The Dump" (the venting area) as a stream of consciousness.
- **Result**: Use the **"Clarify with AI"** button. The system will automatically extract tasks from your venting and put them in your inbox, leaving your mind clear.

### Scenario B: Spontaneous Inspiration
You're in the middle of a meeting and remember you need to buy milk or fix a specific bug.
- **Action**: Hit the **(+) button**, type it, and hit Enter.
- **Result**: It's safely in your **Inbox**. You don't have to break your current focus to remember it later.

### Scenario C: Deep Work Session
You have a complex coding task.
- **Action**: Go to Planning, find the task, and click the **Play (Start)** icon. 
- **Result**: The dashboard switches to **Now View**. Only this task and a timer are visible. When you're done, hit "Complete" to log your win.

### Scenario D: The Weekly Review
It's Sunday and you want to see how you've been spending your time.
- **Action**: Open the **Review** tab.
- **Result**: See a visual breakdown of your focus sessions per domain, helping you adjust your balance for the next week.
