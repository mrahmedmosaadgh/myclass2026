Activity Lesson 7-5 Lesson Overview Determine Outcomes of Compound Events Mathematics Objective Students will be able to: RRuse a tree diagram, a table, or an organized list to represent the sample space for a compound event. Essential Understanding The possible outcomes of a compound event (a combination of two or more events) can be represented using a tree diagram, a table, or an organized list. Previously in this topic, students: • determined the experimental probability of an event. • used probability models to find the probabilities of events. In this lesson, students: • learn the concept of compound events. • use tree diagrams, tables, and organized lists to represent sample spaces. Later in this topic, students will: • determine the probabilities of compound events. • simulate compound events. This lesson emphasizes a blend of conceptual understanding and procedural skills. • Students extend their understanding of events to include compound events. • Students use several methods to represent the sample space of a compound event. Language Support Lesson Language Objective Show and explain how to find all possible outcomes of a compound event. Additional resources are available in the Language Support Handbook. Math Anytime Today’s Challenge Use the Topic 7 problems any time during this topic. FOCUS COHERENCE RIGOR Mathematics Overview Content Standards In this lesson, you will extend students’ understanding of concepts from previous lessons. • 7.SP.C.7 Develop a probability model and use it to find experimental or theoretical probabilities of events. Mathematical Practice Standards MP1 Make Sense and Persevere Students will make sense of the information given in the problems and plan how to best represent the sample space and outcomes of a probability model. MP7 Look for and Make Use of Structure Students will use the structure of tree diagrams, tables, and organized lists to represent all the possible outcomes of compound events. MP8 Generalize Students will recognize that lists, tables, and tree diagrams all show the same sample space for a compound event. Cross-Cluster Connection Students apply their understanding of operations with rational numbers from Topic 1 (7.NS.A) when they determine the outcomes and probabilities of compound events (7.SP.C).
